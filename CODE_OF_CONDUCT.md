# Code of conduct

## Our standards

Project spaces should be welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free. Communicate constructively,
assume good intent, respect different experiences, and focus feedback on the work rather than the
person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate intimidation,
publishing another person's private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when behavior violates these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
